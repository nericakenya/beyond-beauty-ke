Delivery & Refund Policy

Everything you need to know about receiving your order and returning it

Last Updated: 14 June 2026

Beyond Beauty is a trading name of Urembo Luxe Ventures, registered in Kenya. This Delivery & Refund Policy governs all orders placed on beyondbeauty.co.ke. It is drafted in compliance with the Consumer Protection Act, 2012 (Kenya) and the Sale of Goods Act (Cap. 31). Please read this policy carefully before placing your order.

Part A — Delivery

1. Order Processing

On business days (Monday to Friday) orders from within Nairobi are processed within 1 to 3 hours and nationwide within 1 to 3 working days.

Orders placed on weekends and Kenyan public holidays before 12:00 pm EAT will be processed that same day. Orders placed after 12:00 pm will be processed on the next business day.

You will receive an order confirmation via email and/or WhatsApp within 30 minutes of placing your order. A dispatch notification with tracking information will follow once your order is collected by our courier partner.

2. Delivery Areas and Timeframes

Destination Cost Timeframe
Nairobi (all areas) KSh 250 1–3 hours (same day if ordered before 12 pm)
Mombasa (CBD & Nyali) KSh 350 Next business day
Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret KSh 500 1–3 working days
Other counties KSh 500 2–5 working days

Free delivery is offered on all orders above KSh 5,000.

3. Delivery Partners and Tracking Your Order

We partner with trusted courier services including Sendy, G4S Courier and contracted rider networks for Nairobi same-day and nation-wide deliveries. We reserve the right to change delivery partners without notice, provided delivery timeframes are maintained.

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number and the messenger's telephone number via WhatsApp and/or email. You can also check your order status by contacting us directly on WhatsApp at +254 114 481515.

4. Failed Deliveries

If a delivery attempt fails because no one was available to receive it, our courier will leave a notification and attempt re-delivery the following business day. After two failed attempts, the order will be held at our warehouse for 48 hours. If uncollected, the order will be considered as cancelled and the item returned to our stock.

Part B — Returns and Exchanges

5. Our Returns Philosophy

We stand behind everything we sell. If something is not right, we want to make it right. Our returns process is designed to be straightforward, fair and compliant with the Consumer Protection Act, 2012.

6. Eligibility for Returns and Exchanges

6.1 You may return or exchange an item if:

  • The product arrived damaged, defective or materially different from what was described on our website.
  • You received the wrong item.
  • The product is unused and unopened, in its original packaging and in a resalable condition (for skincare, fragrance and hygiene products, the seal must be intact).
  • The return is initiated within 24 hours of delivery.
  • In its original packaging with all accessories, cards and inserts included.
  • Eligible returns are eligible for an exchange or store credit. We do not offer cash refunds for returns.

6.2 Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned or exchanged for hygiene and safety reasons, except where they are defective:

  • Opened skincare, serum or fragrance products (broken seal or removed packaging film).
  • Used makeup items including lip products, mascaras, eyeliners and foundations.
  • Used beauty tools, brushes and accessories.
  • Gift cards and store credit.
  • Items purchased during final sale or clearly marked as non-returnable at point of purchase.

7. How to Initiate a Return

Step 1 — Contact us within 24 hours of delivery via WhatsApp (+254 114 481515) or email (hello@beyondbeauty.co.ke). Include your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason.

Step 2 — We will assess your request and respond within 1 business day with either an approval or an explanation if the item does not qualify.

Step 3 — If approved, we will arrange collection from your original delivery address (Nairobi only, collection at no charge). For upcountry returns, you will need to drop the item at an agreed courier point; we will cover the return courier cost for defective or wrong items.

Step 4 — Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we will process your exchange or store credit within 3 business days.

8. Exchange Methods and Timelines

Scenario Resolution Processing Time
Defective / wrong item Exchange or replacement 3–5 business days
Order cancelled before dispatch Full refund to original payment method 3–5 business days

9. Cancellation of Orders

You may cancel an order before it is dispatched by contacting us on WhatsApp or email. Once dispatched, the order cannot be cancelled but may be returned under Section 6 above.

Pursuant to the Consumer Protection Act, 2012, if you entered into a distance contract (online order), you have the right to cancel within 7 days of receiving the goods without giving a reason, provided the goods are in their original, unused condition.

10. Damaged Goods in Transit

If your order arrives visibly damaged, please photograph the packaging and products immediately before opening and contact us within 24 hours of delivery. We will arrange collection and a replacement at no cost to you.

11. Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy limits or excludes any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act, 2012 or any other applicable Kenyan law. In particular, you retain the statutory right to receive goods that are of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose, and as described.

12. Contact for Delivery & Returns Enquiries

  • WhatsApp: +254 114 481515 (fastest response)
  • Email: hello@beyondbeauty.co.ke
  • Response time: within 1 business day
